Book your clinic visit in under a minute.

Medisync keeps your appointments, records, and reminders in one place, so you spend less time in the waiting room and more time getting care.

What you can book

Pick a service, then choose a doctor and a time that fits your day.

  • General check-up

    Routine exams, consultations, and follow-ups.

  • Pediatrics

    Care for infants, children, and teens.

  • Dental care

    Cleaning, fillings, and dental consultations.

  • Laboratory tests

    Blood work and urinalysis, with results saved to your record.

  • Vaccination

    Flu, hepatitis, and school-required shots.

  • Medical certificates

    Request school and work certificates after your visit.

How it works

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Register once with your basic details to start your patient record.

  2. 2

    Choose a time

    Select a service, a doctor, and an open slot on the calendar.

  3. 3

    Get a reminder

    We confirm your booking and remind you before the visit.
