What you can request

The services our clinic has to offer.

  • General check-up

    Routine exams, consultations, and follow-ups.

  • Pediatrics

    Care for infants, children, and teens.

  • Dental care

    Cleaning, fillings, and dental consultations.

  • Laboratory tests

    Blood work and urinalysis.

  • Vaccination

    Flu, hepatitis, and school-required shots.

  • Medical certificates

    Request school and work certificates after your visit.

How it works

  1. 1

    Log in or register

    Create a free patient account, or log in to start your request.

  2. 2

    Choose a doctor and time

    Pick your preferred doctor, select one of their services, then choose the date and time that suits you.

  3. 3

    Track your request

    The clinic reviews it and confirms or declines. Check 'My Appointments' to see whether it's Pending, Confirmed, or Done.
